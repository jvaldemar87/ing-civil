Who We Are

Over 25 years building trust and solidity in infrastructure.

Historical Review

Since its foundation in 1996, the company already had solid experience in the construction of medium and large-scale infrastructure. Thanks to this accumulated knowledge, its founder, Eng. Hugo Benítez Hernández, decided to launch a business project that would not only participate in civil works execution but also offer maintenance and conservation services. The goal: to guarantee that such works achieve a longer service life, with the quality and safety that users deserve.

This has allowed us to broaden our company's horizons, boosting our participation abroad in highly relevant infrastructure construction and supervision projects. These opportunities have allowed us to know and adapt to diverse and particular forms of control, management, and development of works in contexts different from those of our country.

Currently, the company also provides technical advisory for cost recovery in construction works, in addition to participating in the development of projects in the fields of construction, maintenance, and conservation of buildings, industrial and commercial warehouses. With this, we seek to offer a broader portfolio of services that responds to the needs of the public, as well as public and private sector companies.
